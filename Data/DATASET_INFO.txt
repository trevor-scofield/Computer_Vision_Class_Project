Dataset: HaGRID community subset (100 images per category)
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/GestureDetectionConnoisseurs/hagrid_subsets
Original dataset: https://github.com/hukenovs/hagrid
Subset archive: hagrid-export_100_images.zip (about 402 MB)
Labels: 34 categories, 100 images per category, 3,400 total
Prepared split: Data/hagrid/train (80 per category) and Data/hagrid/test (20 per category)
Sampler seed: 42

The archive is a third-party subset, not an official HaGRID release. The host
labels it CC BY-SA 4.0. Keep HaGRID attribution and review the original
dataset's CC BY-SA 4.0 variant license before redistribution.

Train with:
  python main_gesture.py train --data Data/hagrid --epochs 8

Prepared files are kept locally and excluded from Git by .gitignore because the
image data is large. The archive and extracted source are under Data/downloads
and Data/hagrid_source, respectively.
